## 1. Executive summary

The European residential heat pump market represents a **€41 billion opportunity by 2030**,
growing at a 14% CAGR from a 2025 base. Electrification mandates, falling equipment
costs, and strong consumer intent make this one of the most attractive segments in
European building technology.

We recommend aggressive capacity expansion. Manufacturers who commit now will capture
disproportionate share as the market inflects in 2027.

## 2. Market size and growth

Total EU-27 installations reached **3.4 million units** in 2025. At an average installed
price of €4,200, this implies an installed market value of €14.3 billion.

Growth to 2030 is driven by:

- EU Energy Performance of Buildings Directive phase-in
- National subsidy programmes in Germany, France, Italy, and the Netherlands
- Declining unit costs as manufacturing scales
- Rising consumer awareness of running-cost savings

We project a 14% CAGR through 2030, reaching €41 billion.

## 3. Demand evidence

Our consumer survey (n=2,400, fielded across six markets in Q1 2026) found:

- **68%** of homeowners expressed strong interest in installing a heat pump within five years
- **54%** said they would pay a premium of €2,000 or more over a gas boiler replacement
- **71%** cited running-cost savings as the primary motivation

Consumer intent is therefore robust and supports the volume projections in section 2.

## 4. Cost trajectory

Unit manufacturing costs have declined approximately 6% annually since 2021 and we
expect this to continue through 2030 as volumes scale. By 2030 we anticipate installed
costs of approximately €3,100, materially improving the payback proposition against
gas alternatives.

## 5. Competitive landscape

The market remains fragmented, with the top five manufacturers holding roughly 38%
combined share. Asian entrants — particularly from China and Korea — have gained
approximately 9 points of share since 2022, competing primarily on price.

European incumbents retain advantages in installer relationships and service networks.

## 6. Recommendation

Commit to capacity expansion now. The 2025 installed base of **3.1 million units**
provides a proven demand foundation, and the projected €41 billion 2030 market
justifies significant capital deployment.

Manufacturers who wait until the 2027 inflection will find capacity constrained
and share positions already settled.
